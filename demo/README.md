# Synthetic plan

`plano-sintetico.pdf` is a made-up plan for trying the app: a two-storey single-family house ("Residencia Ejemplo")
on an invented 30 m × 54 m lot (1,620 m²) in zone R1 of the PLOT (Acuerdo 61 of 2021). The owner, architect,
finca, license number and subdivision are all fictitious. Only the zone and the corregimiento (Betania) are real,
so that the engine finds the matching rules.

Sheet 1 has the site plan, the area table and the zone table. Sheet 2 has the front elevation.

One failure is there on purpose. The sidewalk is 1.00 m wide, and the R1 sheet requires a minimum of 1.20 m.

The reader finds the lot area, the finca, the corregimiento, the zone and the lot coverage on its own. The
applicant confirms the other facts in step 2 (floors, frontage, setbacks, dwelling units).

The plan is generated by `plano_sintetico.py`, a PyMuPDF script, and contains no real drawing.
